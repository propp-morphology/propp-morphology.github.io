# Propp's Tale Schema

One page on Vladimir Propp's «Морфология сказки» (Leningrad, 1928): the 31 functions in their order, his symbols
(1928, with the letters of the 1968 English edition), the spheres of action of the seven dramatis personae, and how
moves combine, with his own words and page for each. English and Russian: `index.html#en`, `index.html#ru`.

The worked example is Propp's analysis of «Гуси-лебеди» (Afanasyev No. 113). Counts of how often each function occurs
come from Propp's tables of 1928 and 1969.

Built from the project's data (`scripts/propp/schema_page/`: `gen_data.py` and `template.html`): Propp 1928 in the FEB
transcription, his tables read from the 1928 print and the 1969 Наука print, and the 1968 English edition
(trans. L. Scott, rev. L. A. Wagner) for the English terms.
