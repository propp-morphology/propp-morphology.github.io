# Propp's Tale Schema

https://propp-morphology.github.io/ (English) · https://propp-morphology.github.io/ru/ (Russian)

An interactive visualisation of Vladimir Propp's *Morphology of the Folktale* (Leningrad, 1928): all 31 functions in
Propp's order, their pairs and spheres of action, how moves combine, and Propp's own analyses of 13 wonder tales from
Afanasyev's collection. For "The Swan-Geese" (No. 113) each symbol of the formula is linked to its passage in the tale.

Sources, named in full in the page's footer: Propp's 1928 text (FEB) for the Russian page and the Russian originals;
the 1968 English edition (University of Texas Press) for the English quotations, names and symbols; Propp's tables of
1928 (our transcription from the scan) and 1969; Afanasyev's tales in the 1984–85 Nauka edition (FEB).

## How the site is made

The pages here are generated, not written by hand. A build script assembles each language from one template and the
project's data, pre-renders it in headless Chrome (so the text is in the HTML for search engines) and writes the share
images, icons and sitemap. The source lives in the project's working repository, not here: to propose a change, open an
issue or a pull request against the files here, and we will carry it over to the source and rebuild.
